Case 03 · Featured capstone · Full-stack development

Tap to Trade

Connecting local players,
one trade at a time.

A marketplace for Magic: The Gathering players to share bulk inventories, search by card and city, and arrange in-person trades. My final-year capstone brings inventory management, offers and moderation into one web application.

The problem

Finding nearby players with the right cards in their bulk collections.

The approach

Searchable inventories, counter-offers, role-based moderation and report handling.

02 / The suspect

Behind the work

The person
behind the commits.

Hamilton, late hours. A compiler error and a cup of cold coffee. Somewhere in between, a solution takes shape.

I’m a software developer based in Hamilton, Ontario, with a background in software development at Mohawk College. I work across full-stack web applications, games and visual media.

I write clean, testable code and see projects through to deployment. I enjoy the details: tracing a bug, shaping an interaction, or connecting a front end to the data behind it.

Outside the code, I make videos, edit photos and produce short films. That attention to visual storytelling carries into the software and games I build.
